# Wave 2 handoff — 2026-09-30

## Delivered

Wave 2 adds provider-aware domain and universe onboarding to the Wave 1 product
foundation. A user can describe a domain, configure selection parameters, inspect
provider readiness, generate a scored and diversified company basket, replace
eligible companies, and atomically lock the universe into a durable workspace.

The provider layer now has typed contracts for discovery, mapping, market data,
fundamentals, corporate actions, calendars, FX, and evidence. Implemented adapters
cover EODHD global search/data, OpenFIGI mapping, and Alpha Vantage compatibility.
All normal tests use frozen fixtures and require no network or credentials.
