# Initial Research Hypothesis

## Filing language drift

Changes in a company's forward-looking and risk language between consecutive SEC filings may add information beyond price-only features. This is a hypothesis, not a claim of predictive alpha.

## Planned evaluation

- Ingest filings with their actual public availability timestamp.
- Compare the same filing sections across consecutive periods.
- Evaluate the feature only in locked walk-forward periods against price-only baselines.
- Record the complete trial count, costs, and negative results.

## Exclusions

The first evaluation will not scrape unlicensed data, use future filings, publish investment recommendations, or connect to a broker.
